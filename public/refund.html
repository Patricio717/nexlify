If you're not satisfied with your purchase, we offer a 30-day refund guarantee. No questions asked.

Email support@nexlify.com with your order number and reason for the refund. We'll process it within 3-5 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.

Exceptions

Refunds may be denied if:
